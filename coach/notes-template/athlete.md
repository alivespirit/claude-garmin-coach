As of YYYY-MM-DD. Lasting facts about the athlete; the coach updates this file when it learns
something new. Any language works: write it in the language you'll talk to the coach in.
Until it's filled in, the coach relies on Garmin data.

## Schedule and logistics

- Preferred training time and workday constraints.
- Regular activities that don't need analysis (e.g. short commute walks).
- Current weekly grid (details in `plan.md`).
- Known breaks: travel, visits, holidays.

## Locations and routes

- Area, surfaces and elevation. The weather location for activities without GPS is set in
  `config.env`.
- Route habits (e.g. explores new trails, so long runs often end up longer than planned).
- GPS quirks in the area, if any.
- Seasonal constraints (e.g. no treadmill, winter conditions).

## Fueling

- Experience with gels, bottle capacity, pre-workout breakfast and caffeine.

## Feedback preferences

- **Language and tone:** e.g. English, informal.
- What a good analysis looks like (verdict first, per-rep numbers, comparison with last week,
  "why" and then "what next").
- Things to avoid (e.g. don't cap distance when the run feels good; check facts first).
- Calendar changes: propose them, and make them only after a "yes".
